## Design Context

### Users
High school students (14–18) at Little Elm High School browsing from phones and laptops; prospective members and parents researching the club; officers managing chapter logistics. Primary context: school setting, daytime browsing, professional audience including parents and teachers.

### Brand Personality
Official · Driven · Sharp

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean & official — matches the national TSA brand identity. White background with TSA Blue (#005DAA) and TSA Red (#EE2624) as the only accent colors. No gradients, no glassmorphism, no decorative noise. Feels like it belongs alongside the national TSA website. Light mode is default; dark mode available via toggle.

### Typography
- Headings / subheads / UI chrome: Proxima Nova → Franklin Gothic Medium fallback
- Body copy / paragraphs: Source Sans 3 (Source Sans Pro)
- Franklin Gothic as Microsoft/fallback substitute per TSA Brand Guide

### Color Palette
Primary:
- TSA Blue: #005DAA (Pantone 286)
- TSA Red: #EE2624 (Pantone 1795)
- White: #FFFFFF

Secondary (accent only):
- Sky: #4E8AC9 | Green: #86BB50 | Yellow: #FFC425 | Orange: #EC881D
- Purple: #574E8F | Maroon: #9C1D20 | Cobalt: #006FA6
- Navy: #324659 | Charcoal: #4D4D4F | Silver: #D5DCE0

### Design Principles
1. **TSA Blue is the hero** — it anchors every interactive element; red is used sparingly for urgency/CTAs only
2. **Typography carries the hierarchy** — weight and size create structure, not colored backgrounds or decorative borders
3. **Uniform background** — pages are white in light mode; no alternating section tints; structure comes from spacing and typography
4. **Footer is TSA Blue** — bookends the page with brand authority
5. **No AI tells** — no gradient text, no border-left accent stripes, no glowing card borders as decoration
